# Knowledge Article: KB-1004 - Physical Security Badge and RFID Access

## Scope

Applies to physical employee smartcards, RFID turnstile badges, and office building perimeter access.

## Common Symptoms

- Turnstiles flash red when badging into the facility
- Access denied to specific secure server rooms or floor wings
- NFC card unreadable or demagnetized

## Approved Self-Service Steps

1. **Physical Card Integrity Check**:
   - Inspect the smart badge for physical cracks, bends, or damage to the embedded copper antenna wire.
   - Remove badge from thick metal RFID-blocking sleeves or proximity to magnetic smartphone cases before scanning.
2. **Access Clearance Status Self-Check**:
   - Log into the internal employee portal `https://security.corporate.internal/badge-status`.
   - Verify that your annual security training compliance is up to date (non-compliance automatically suspends physical access after 14 days).
3. **Manager Approval Re-Verification**:
   - If requesting access to restricted areas (Lab 4, Data Center, Executive Boardroom), confirm that your line manager submitted the departmental access request form.

## Escalation Path

If the card is intact and portal status is active but scanner flashes red, route ticket to **IT-Identity-Access-Management** and include employee ID and badge serial number.
